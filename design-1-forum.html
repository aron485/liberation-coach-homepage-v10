Questions

Frequently asked

Are sessions online? +
Yes. Coaching is delivered online by Victor, so you can work together from wherever you are.
Who is it for? +
Founders, business owners and adult athletes aged 18 and older who want to work on how they respond to a specific challenge.
What happens after I apply? +
Applying opens a no-obligation discovery Zoom call with Victor — a conversation to see if the work fits, not a booked session and not automatic acceptance.
What is Circle access? +
All coaching members receive access to Circle alongside their 1:1 work; your coach will walk through what that includes when you begin.
Is coaching the same as therapy? +
No. Performance coaching is focused on a present situation and the next step you want to take; it isn’t therapy or medical treatment.
