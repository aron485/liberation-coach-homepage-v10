The Method

What you can work on with Victor

In performance coaching, you and Victor work together on those questions. Personal growth can mean a change you choose in your own life, such as asking a question you have avoided, checking an assumption or taking an action you chose. You can then review what you did and what you learned.

Reflection and emotional awareness. Victor's emotional-awareness teaching includes noticing bodily sensations associated with a feeling. His reflective questions examine the situation and your interpretation of it. The Method page explains these parts of his work in plain language.

Three reflective questions shape the work. Open each for an example. These are examples, not a fixed script.

1What happened?

Example: describe the situation plainly. After a difficult conversation, note what you said and how the other person responded.

2What are you assuming it means?

Example: name the interpretation you are adding. If you keep revisiting a decision, separate the information you still need from an assumption you are making.

3What do you need to find out before deciding?

Example: identify a question to ask or an assumption to check before you act, then choose one action to try and review what happened.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Coaching is delivered online by Victor, so you can work together from wherever you are.

Who is it for?

Founders, business owners and adult athletes aged 18 and older who want to work on how they respond to a specific challenge.

What happens after I apply?

The next step is a discovery Zoom call with Victor. There is no obligation to proceed with coaching.

What is Circle access?

All coaching members receive access to Circle alongside their 1:1 work; your coach will walk through what that includes when you begin.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Performance coaching is focused on a present situation and the next step you want to take; it isn't therapy or medical treatment.
